Wellness Take Home

Continue the journey at home.

The wellness shelf is not a medicine cabinet and not a souvenir shelf. It is the at-home continuation of Chinese seasonal living: tea, incense, ritual cards, Thangka learning materials, and product guides that keep the traveler connected before or after China.

Tea and seasonal rhythm

Daily China at home.

Tea cards, solar-term notes, tasting sets, and food rhythm prompts introduce Chinese seasonal living through ordinary, beautiful daily habits.

Incense and scent ritual

Fragrance as culture.

Sachets, incense plaques, fragrance beads, and cultural guide cards framed as personal ritual, spatial aesthetics, and memory of place.

Thangka and craft learning

Learn the context first.

Line-study sheets, pigment stories, kit videos, and future studio routes help you understand the culture before you make or collect.

Before and after travel

Let the journey begin before departure.

Some guests start with a card or a small kit. Some begin with a gift box. Some return home and want to keep learning. Take Home keeps the place close before, during, and after the trip.

Journey Path

From first curiosity to a deeper route.

Before travel: free Chinese name card, starter gift, material kit with video guide when included, and waitlist.

During travel: Ruiyun royal fragrance retreat, Great Wall foothills, Beijing palace aesthetics, Central Axis, fan, writing, seal, and rubbing workshops; Jiangnan scent and handwork; Fujian or Yunnan tea routes; Western Sichuan Thangka studio, guide, car, photographer, and cultural interpretation.

After travel: reorder kits, gift boxes, corporate cultural gifts, separate workshop inquiries, and return-route planning.
