About Tsingpu Cultural Travel

A stay can become a deeper way to see China.

Tsingpu is a cultural-retreat brand known for bringing local architecture, landscape, food, craft, and everyday life into the guest experience. Discover Real China draws on this place-based way of traveling when it helps guests choose where to stay and what to experience.

The idea
Since 2015

The name Tsingpu points to a return to what is original: the relationship between a place, the people who live there, and the rhythm of everyday life.

For guests, that can mean a stay with more to notice: architecture, landscape, food, craft, local stories, and objects to take home.
