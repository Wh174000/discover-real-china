Three ways to begin

One Shanghai arrival, three ways into Jiangnan.

Your time can be as short as half a day or as long as five days. Choose one place to begin, or combine the three experiences; each offers a different way to experience Jiangnan.

01 / CityShanghai Urban Wellness

Best for a half day or one day: Bund, former-concession streets, design neighborhoods, tea, seasonal food, incense welcome, private dining, and a soft first explanation of Chinese living culture.

02 / VillageLiyao Village Reset

Best for one day or one quiet overnight: fields, renovated rural architecture, small studios, incense or sachet craft, tea, slow food, photography, and non-medical TCM rhythm.

03 / JiangnanSuzhou & Yangzhou Culture

Best for two to three days: Suzhou gardens and water, Yangzhou canal life and food, silk-inspired craft, handwork, tea, and cultural stays shaped around gardens and water.

Distinct meanings

Three places, three clear roles.

Each place offers something different. Shanghai is modern entry and urban wellness. Liyao is village calm and scent-handwork. Suzhou and Yangzhou are Jiangnan aesthetics, gardens, canals, food, and traditional cultural refinement.

Shanghai / modern TCM living

Tea, food, scent, city rhythm

Shanghai creates easy arrival, trust, modern Chinese life, seasonal tea, incense welcome, food pacing, and a gentle cultural interpretation before guests go deeper.

Liyao / rural reset

Fields, craft, quiet overnight

Liyao Village creates the strongest city-rural contrast: field edges, renovated architecture, sachets or incense, handwork, slow meals, and a slower pause away from the city.

Suzhou & Yangzhou / Jiangnan depth

Gardens, canals, refined life

Suzhou and Yangzhou fit guests who want traditional culture with more texture: garden thinking, canal mornings, old streets, craft, food, tea, and cultural stays.
