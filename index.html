Beijing

The first gateway.

Beijing introduces Real China through a clear first product: palace scale and hutong life in the city, then a 2 to 3 day cultural retreat anchored by Ruiyun Winery, the Great Wall foothills, fragrance bead making, Pankou craft, and dinner-table memory.

Palace scale, hutong life, retreat idea

Signature journeys

Beijing feels easy to say yes to. Two recommended routes reduce choice stress while keeping the trip custom, and both start from the same Real contrast: the grand order of the capital and the human scale of daily life.

Great Wall Wine Retreat2 to 3 days at Ruiyun with vineyards, Great Wall light, courtyard dinners, and one heritage experience.
Beijing First-Time Cultural EntryHutongs, tea, roast duck, contemporary city scenes, and a soft landing into Chinese culture.
Ruiyun as the physical anchor
The Great Wall turns Beijing into a retreat
Hutong doorways keep the story human

Local experiences

  • Fragrance bead making as a Beijing scent-craft experience.
  • Teacher-led Pankou knot workshop with wearable knot forms.
  • Hutong walk, roast duck, and contemporary Beijing contrast.
  • Private photo session at the Great Wall foothills.

Take home

Royal fragrance sets, calligraphy name tags, seal stories, and Beijing craft kits let the journey begin before visitors are ready to travel. Material kits may include video guides; teacher-led workshops are separate experiences.

What you will experience

Beijing brings together a real stay, a landmark with history, everyday hutong life, and hands-on experiences that can continue at home.

The Real contrast

The palace image gives Beijing scale, color, and ceremony. The hutong image gives shade, neighbors, shops, and ordinary life. Together they make Beijing feel less like a monument and more like a journey.

Shanghai and Jiangnan Villages

From skyline to village life.

Shanghai is the modern gateway. After landing, guests can choose three Jiangnan directions: urban wellness in Shanghai, a Liyao Village reset, or Suzhou and Yangzhou culture. Short stays can begin with one place; longer stays can combine all three.

Riverfront energy, village calm, craft life

Signature journeys

Shanghai is more than a checklist. It starts with skyline confidence, then reveals rural Jiangnan life that overseas travelers rarely expect so close to the city.

Shanghai Urban WellnessHalf day or one day for Bund, former-concession streets, tea, seasonal food, incense welcome, private dining, and modern Chinese living culture.
Liyao Village ResetOne day or one quiet overnight for fields, renovated rural architecture, craft studios, incense or sachet handwork, tea, slow food, and photography.
Suzhou & Yangzhou CultureTwo to three days for gardens, canal life, old streets, refined food, silk-inspired handwork, tea, and cultural stays.
Liyao Village adds renovated rural architecture and quiet cultural stays

Local experiences

  • Bund and former concession architecture walk.
  • Contemporary Chinese design and independent retail.
  • Private dining, coffee, and night photography.
  • One-day or overnight Liyao village extension for fields, waterways, incense, tea, and craft studios.
  • Suzhou and Yangzhou extensions for gardens, canal mornings, food, handwork, tea, and Jiangnan cultural stays.
Hands, incense, objects and slow interior light create the craft story

Jiangnan, made easy to enter

Liyao Village gives Shanghai a gentle change of pace: city, fields, village architecture, scent, tea, and handwork. Choose Shanghai, Liyao, Suzhou, or Yangzhou according to your time, interests, and budget, then let us help arrange the order.

Take home

What the journey feels like

The city-rural contrast is easier to understand than simply adding more stops after Shanghai. It gives the traveler a clear emotional arc: global speed, then nearby rural calm.

Chengdu and Western Sichuan

Tea tables in the city. Glacier peaks beyond it.

Chengdu is the soft landing. Western Sichuan is the emotional pull: tea houses and pandas in the city, then Hailuogou glaciers, Yading valleys, Tibetan highland culture, hot springs, three reachable hotel stays, and Thangka stories that can be discovered before travel. Chongqing sits close enough to become a natural custom extension when guests want one more cinematic city.

Tea house city, glacier country, one journey

Signature journeys

This route links city arrival, highland landscapes, cultural learning, and objects you can take home in one journey.

Glacier Hot Springs4 days / 3 nights from Chengdu to Hailuogou for glacier light, forest roads, hot springs, and a manageable first Western Sichuan route.
Garze Thangka Country4 days into Garze for Gesar stories, Thangka ateliers, mineral pigments, creator context, and a calmer highland rhythm.
Tibetan Highlands Explorer7 to 8 days from Chengdu into Hailuogou, Garze, Daocheng Yading, Tibetan culture, and an English-guided highland route.

Chongqing extension

When you want one more cinematic city, Chongqing can add one or two nights of vertical city views, river crossings, hotpot, night photography, and mountain-city street life before or after Chengdu.

Take home

Continue the Thangka story with limited prints, pigment and line-study kits, video lessons, online studio visits, and later cultural travel. The story is not only the finished image: rare mineral colors, years of handwork, and the discipline of the artisan make the object meaningful before the traveler ever reaches Ganzi.

Quanzhou

The port of living heritage.

Quanzhou carries a maritime China story through temples, trade routes, Minnan food, tea, stone streets, craft workshops, and layered family culture. It opens a coastal, historic, highly visual southern journey.

Tulou extension, communal architecture, southern living heritage

Signature journeys

Quanzhou is framed as a maritime heritage and living food culture route, not a static old-town visit.

Maritime Silk Road City WalkTemples, old streets, port memory, craft stops, tea, snacks, and evening photography.
Quanzhou To Fujian Tulou ExtensionA deeper southern China route pairing coastal culture with communal architecture and mountain tea life.
Inside the round house, the route becomes daily life

Local experiences

  • Minnan snacks, tea, puppet theatre, and local opera.
  • Temple architecture, incense culture, and old street photography.
  • Optional Tulou extension for family architecture and mountain village life.

Take home

Tea, incense, puppet-inspired objects, craft kits, Tulou models, and Fujian photography prints let a coastal city story continue at home.

What you will take away

Quanzhou adds a southern maritime story through food, faith, trade, craft, and objects that can keep the journey present at home.

Traveler FAQ

Practical questions before you choose a route.

Travel details vary by season, city, payment method, and your route. We help you confirm the practical details before you book or pay.

Do I need a visa?

Visa, transit, and entry rules change. For each inquiry, we check the current official requirements for your passport, route, and dates before confirming a plan.

How do overseas guests pay in China?

Card acceptance, mobile payment setup, deposits, and payment methods vary by route. We confirm the options before booking and explain what needs to be prepared before arrival.

Can you help with internet, maps, and apps?

Yes. For confirmed travelers we prepare a pre-arrival checklist covering eSIM or roaming, map access, translation tools, ride-hailing, and the apps most useful for the chosen cities.

Are the journeys private or fixed group tours?

You can ask for a full private plan or just help with a hotel, car day, scenic spot, cultural experience, tea seat, shopping lead, or a free half day. We confirm dates, group size, pace, service level, and availability before anything is treated as confirmed.

Are wellness experiences medical services?

No. These experiences are cultural and educational, not diagnosis, treatment, clinical care, or a promise of health outcomes.

How do Take Home products ship?

We confirm availability, batch timing, materials, customs rules, and shipping cost before payment. Some products may start as waitlist, quote, or class-access only.
